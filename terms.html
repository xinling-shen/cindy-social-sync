Cindy Social Sync is a private internal reporting tool that connects an authorized TikTok account and syncs available public profile and video metrics to a private Feishu spreadsheet.

Private Internal Reporting Use

This app is intended only for private internal reporting and record keeping by the app owner. It is not offered as a public service or commercial data product.

TikTok Authorization

The user authorizes TikTok access through TikTok Login Kit. The app only accesses TikTok account data after the relevant TikTok account grants authorization through TikTok's official authorization flow.

Data Handling

Data accessed through the app is used only to sync available public profile and video metrics to a private Feishu reporting spreadsheet.

Data is not sold, published, or shared with third parties.

Revoking Authorization

The user can revoke authorization at any time through TikTok account settings or any other authorization management tools provided by TikTok.

Changes

These terms may be updated as needed to reflect changes to the private internal reporting tool or its data usage.
