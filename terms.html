Cindy Creator Showcase is a web app that lets TikTok creators connect their own TikTok account, view their public creator profile and public videos, and choose which videos to display on a creator showcase page.

Creator Showcase Use

The app is intended for creators who want to review and display their own authorized public TikTok profile information and public videos.

TikTok Authorization

Users authorize TikTok access through TikTok Login Kit. The app only accesses TikTok account data after the account owner grants authorization through TikTok's official authorization flow.

User Control

Creators can choose which authorized public videos appear in their showcase preview. Users can stop using the app or revoke TikTok authorization at any time through TikTok account settings or any other authorization management tools provided by TikTok.

Data Handling

Data accessed through the app is used to display the user's authorized public profile and selected public videos in the creator showcase experience.

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with data brokers or advertising networks.

Changes

These terms may be updated as needed to reflect changes to the app, its features, or its data usage.
