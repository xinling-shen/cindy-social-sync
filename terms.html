Cindy Creator Studio is a public user-facing creator tool that lets TikTok creators connect their own TikTok account, view basic account stats and public videos, upload their own videos to TikTok as drafts, and share selected creator content.

Creator Tool Use

The app is intended for creators who want to review their own authorized TikTok account stats, review public video performance, prepare video draft uploads, and share selected creator content through TikTok. Users are responsible for the videos and captions they provide.

TikTok Authorization

Users authorize TikTok access through TikTok Login Kit. The app only accesses TikTok account data after the account owner grants authorization through TikTok's official authorization flow.

Products and Scopes

The app demonstrates Login Kit, Content Posting API, and Share Kit. It may request the following TikTok scopes when needed for the creator workflow: user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.list, and video.upload.

User Control

Uploaded videos are sent to TikTok as drafts, and the creator continues editing and publishing inside TikTok. Users can stop using the app or revoke TikTok authorization at any time through TikTok account settings or other authorization management tools provided by TikTok.

Data Handling

Data accessed through the app is used to display the user's authorized TikTok basic account data, account stats, public video performance, upload draft status, and Share Kit demo status inside the creator studio experience.

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with data brokers or advertising networks.

Changes

These terms may be updated as needed to reflect changes to the app, its features, or its data usage.
