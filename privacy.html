Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 20, 2026

Cindy Creator Showcase lets TikTok creators connect their own TikTok account, view their public creator profile and public videos, and choose which videos to display on a creator showcase page.

Data Accessed

When authorized by the TikTok account owner, the app may access the following TikTok data:

  • Profile link
  • Bio
  • Verification status
  • Follower count
  • Following count
  • Likes count
  • Video count
  • Public video list
  • Captions
  • Video URLs
  • Publish time
  • View count
  • Like count
  • Comment count
  • Share count

How Data Is Used

Data is used to show the creator their authorized public TikTok profile and public videos, and to let the creator choose which public videos to display on a showcase page.

Data Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with data brokers or advertising networks. The showcase feature only displays public TikTok profile and video information selected by the authorized creator.

User Control

Users can stop using the app or revoke TikTok authorization at any time through TikTok account settings or any other authorization management tools provided by TikTok.
