Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 20, 2026

Cindy Creator Studio lets TikTok creators connect their own TikTok account, view basic account stats and public videos, upload their own videos to TikTok as drafts, and share selected creator content.

Data Accessed

When authorized by the TikTok account owner, the app may access the following TikTok data:

  • Display name
  • Avatar
  • Open ID
  • Follower count
  • Following count
  • Likes count
  • Video count
  • Public video list
  • Captions
  • Video URLs
  • Publish time
  • View count
  • Like count
  • Comment count
  • Share count
  • Creator-selected upload file name and caption in the upload draft demo

How Data Is Used

Data is used to show the creator their authorized TikTok basic account data, account stats, public video performance, draft upload demo status, and Share Kit demo status.

TikTok Products and Scopes

The app uses Login Kit for account authorization, Content Posting API for video draft upload, and Share Kit for sharing selected content through TikTok. The app may request user.info.basic, user.info.stats, video.list, and video.upload for these workflows.

Data Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with data brokers or advertising networks. Public TikTok video information may be displayed inside the creator studio experience for the authorized creator.

User Control

Creators choose what video draft to upload and what selected content to share through TikTok. Users can stop using the app or revoke TikTok authorization at any time through TikTok account settings or any other authorization management tools provided by TikTok.
